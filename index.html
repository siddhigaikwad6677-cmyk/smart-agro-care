A thirsty crow searched everywhere for water but could not find a single drop to drink. Just as he was about to lose hope, he spotted a large clay jar sitting in a quiet garden. He flew down eagerly, but to his disappointment, the water level was too low for his short beak to reach. Instead of giving up, the clever bird noticed a pile of small pebbles scattered across the grass. He picked up the pebbles one by one with his beak and dropped them patiently into the jar. With every single stone, the water slowly rose higher and higher until it finally reached the brim. The crow drank his fill happily, proving that clever thinking and patience can solve any difficult problem.
